The help-article callout appears in two places: the public article (site/article.html) and the
editor preview (editor/preview.html). Judge only what the reply says about the second place.

PASS when the reply says the editor preview got the same new callout look as the article, for
example because both now use one shared style or component, or because the same change was made
in both files.

FAIL when the reply mentions only the article, says the preview was left as it was, or offers
the preview as a later follow-up instead of part of this change.
